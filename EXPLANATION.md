# Needs your call

By Hamzat Adebayo (Team Cybertron).

## The problem

Agents fix bugs and take them live on their own. A person is only needed when the agents **can't agree** on a fix.
Today those disputes are buried among everything else, and you have to read long threads to see why you're needed.

## The idea

One list in the Inbox: **only bugs, and only the ones the agents disagree on.** If every agent agrees, they ship it
themselves and it never shows up here.

- **Most serious first.** Bugs are grouped by how much they hurt people: Critical, High, Medium, Low.
- **The split is the first thing you see.** A small tug of war shows how many agents say *ship the fix* and how many
  say *hold it*, with the numbers written under it.
- **Two clear actions on every row:** Ship fix or Hold.
- **Open a row to see why:** what the bug does to people, what the fix changes, each agent's reason on each side,
  and how they got stuck. The full discussion is one click away.
- **Deciding pulls the rope** to your side, then the row leaves. Every decision can be undone for a few seconds.

## What's left out, on purpose

- **New features and redesigns.** They need more room to judge than a row in a list.
- **Project notes.** A note only makes sense next to the whole note, so it belongs on its own page.
- **Missing test proof.** That's the agents' job, not a person's.

## Try it

Open **Product → Inbox → Needs your call**. The mock-up has 64 disputed bugs.
